## Part 1: How We Connect (Platforms & Tools)
Before the schedule begins, you need the right setup to ensure a two-way connection, rather than just a one-way broadcast.
*   **The Broadcast:** YouTube Live or Twitch (for high-quality screen sharing and slides).
*   **The Hub:** The **GPT4Free Discord Server**. Create a temporary category called `🎉 Community Day 2026`.
    *   `#live-chat`: For hype during the stream.
    *   `#q-and-a`: Where users drop questions for the 21:00 CET session.
    *   `#showcase-your-projects`: Let users post what they’ve built using g4f.
*   **The Interactive Tool:** Use **Mentimeter** or **Slido** during the opening to generate live,
      on-screen graphics based on community input (like a live world map showing where everyone is from).

## Part 3: Extra Ideas to Maximize Community Connection

To truly make it feel like a *community* and not just a lecture, implement these ideas throughout the day:

1.  **"Show Us Your Setup" Discord Thread:** Ask users to post screenshots of how they use GPT4Free (their IDE, their custom UIs, their homelab servers). Heiner can show the best ones live on stream.
2.  **Collaborative Bug Hunt / Hackathon:** Announce a 24-hour window following the stream where anyone who submits a valid PR to the new `pa-providers` repo gets a special "Community Day 2026" Discord role.
3.  **Easter Eggs in v7.7.7:** Hide a fun easter egg in the new System Tray or Logs Inspector. The first community member to find it and post it in chat gets a shoutout. 
4.  **Community Contributor Awards:** Take 5 minutes during the stream to publicly thank 3-5 non-admin community members who have been incredibly helpful in the Discord or GitHub issues over the last year.
